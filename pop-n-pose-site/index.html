Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

How much space do you need? +

A 10x10 ft footprint covers the booth and backdrop comfortably. We can work with less — just let us know the room when you book.

Do you travel outside Toronto? +

Yes — we regularly cover Mississauga, Vaughan, Oakville and the broader GTA. Venues outside a 30km radius may include a small travel fee.

How far in advance should we book? +

Wedding season (May–October) books up 3–6 months out. For showers and corporate events, 3–4 weeks' notice is usually plenty.

Is a deposit required? +

A 30% deposit holds your date, with the balance due one week before your event. We'll send a contract and invoice once details are confirmed.

Can guests get digital copies too? +

Every session lands in a shareable online gallery, plus a QR code at the booth so guests can grab their digital copy on the spot.
