Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Something else on your mind? Send it over with your date and we'll answer that too.

How much space do you need?

A 10 × 10 ft footprint covers the booth and backdrop. Tighter room? Share the details when you book and we'll make it work.

Do you travel outside Toronto?

All the time: Mississauga, Vaughan, Oakville and the broader GTA. Venues beyond about 30 km may carry a small travel fee.

How far in advance should we book?

Wedding season (May to October) books 3 to 6 months out. Showers and corporate events usually need 3 to 4 weeks' notice.

Is a deposit required?

A 30% deposit holds your date, and the balance is due one week before the event. We send a contract and invoice once the details are confirmed.

Can guests get digital copies too?

Yes! Every session lands in a shareable online gallery, and a QR code at the booth lets guests grab their copy on the spot.
